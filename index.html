Spotify Sentiment Intelligence

Turns app-store reviews into a themed escalation queue, so product and support know which unhappy customers to answer first.

  • Python
  • LLM classification
  • Airtable
  • NPS
What I built
A review taxonomy built with a large model, then 401 Spotify reviews classified one by one for theme, sentiment band and urgency, feeding a live dashboard with an escalation queue.
Result
37% of reviews needed a human response, and playback reliability drove 37.7% of all detractors, which set the fix-it order.

UAE Credit Card Intelligence

  • Python
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • Streamlit
What I built
A deterministic calculation engine for rewards, fees, caps and eligibility. The language model only explains the result; it never does the maths.
Why it's trustworthy
Every card rule comes from the issuer's official documents, with source and version tracked. Anything unverifiable is marked unknown, not guessed.

Customer Intelligence Engine

Predicts which customers will generate the most future revenue, so marketing budget goes where it pays back.

  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • CLV
  • Churn
What I built
A modular churn and customer-value pipeline on the UCI Online Retail II dataset: 4,966 customers, 180-day prediction window.
Result
The top 10% of customers by predicted value captured 69.9% of actual future revenue. Error fell from £886 (baseline) to £617 per customer.

From marketing results to AI systems

I've always been fascinated by questions like:

  • Why do customers do what they do?
  • Why does a campaign resonate with one audience but not another?
  • Why does one customer become a loyal advocate while another quietly disappears?
  • And how can technology help businesses better understand, engage, and create lasting value for their customers?

Those questions sparked my interest in analytics early in my career and eventually led me into marketing, loyalty, and artificial intelligence.

As a Customer Analytics, Loyalty, and AI Leader with over a decade of experience across retail, e-commerce, and customer engagement, I help organizations understand customer behavior, predict future actions, identify growth opportunities, and create experiences that are more relevant, personalized, and valuable for customers.

My journey began in Business Intelligence and Analytics, where I learned that data alone rarely changes outcomes. Every purchase, every click, every visit, every abandoned cart tells a story. What changes outcomes is turning those insights into actions that drive business results.

Over the years, I've built my career around uncovering those stories and helping organizations make better decisions. I am passionate about simplifying complexity, connecting customer needs with business objectives, and helping organizations make smarter decisions through data and AI.

As artificial intelligence became more accessible, I expanded my focus to applying AI and machine learning to real business challenges. From customer churn prediction and recommendation systems to marketing optimization and AI-powered decision workflows, my work has centered on helping teams move from reporting what happened to predicting what happens next and recommending the best course of action.
